CASE 2  A genuine or likely contradiction
================================================================================================
  Same entity, measure, period, basis and unit dimension - and different numbers, with nothing
  in either frame to account for the gap.

  lemma  : net FDI
  rule   : CONTRADICTS   verdict: contradicts

  reading A  10.1 USD billion   [FY24]
             Economic Survey 2024-25  published 2025-01  p.66
             “For FY24 as a whole, the net FDI was USD 10.1 billion.”

  reading B  26.8 US$ billion   [2023-24]

             “| 1.1 Net Inward FDI (1.1.1 - 1.1.2) | 56.2 | 42.0 | 26.8 | 29.6 |”

  reasoning:
    Same entity, measure, period (FY24) and basis, but Economic Survey 2024-25 (undated)
    reports 10.1 USD billion and ANNUAL REPORT FOR THE YEAR 2024-25 (undated) reports 26.8
    US$ billion - a 62.3% difference with nothing in either frame to account for it.

  escalated -> model says contradicts (UNRESOLVED_DISCREPANCY):
    The Economic Survey reports net FDI as USD 10.1 billion for FY24, while the Annual
    Report lists it as 26.8 US$ billion without providing sufficient methodological context
    in the text to reconcile the substantial gap.

CASE 3  An apparent contradiction explained by context
================================================================================================
  The values differ, but so does one coordinate of the frame - the period, the reporting
  basis, or the stage of revision. That coordinate IS the explanation.

  lemma  : Revenue from Operations (Standalone)
  rule   : BASIS_MISMATCH   verdict: reconciled

  reading A  74,540.82 ₹ Million   [FY24]
             Annual Report 2023-24  published None  p.22
             “Revenue from Operations 74,540.82 66,586.61 81,415.38 72,253.01”

  reading B  81,415.38 ₹ Million   [FY24]
             Annual Report 2023-24  published None  p.22
             “Revenue from Operations 74,540.82 66,586.61 81,415.38 72,253.01”

  reasoning:
    Different reporting basis: standalone against consolidated. These are different figures
    by construction, not competing measurements of one figure.
