CASE 2  A genuine or likely contradiction
================================================================================================
  Same entity, measure, period, basis and unit dimension - and different numbers, with nothing
  in either frame to account for the gap.

  lemma  : Increase/(Decrease) in the average salaries of employees other than managerial personnel
  rule   : CONTRADICTS   verdict: contradicts

  reading A  14 per cent   [FY24]
             Annual Report 2023-24  published None  p.34
             “Increase/(Decrease) in the average salaries of employees other than managerial personnel in the FY24 was 14%.”

  reading B  (6.97) per cent   [FY24]
             Annual Report 2023-24  published None  p.34
             “Increase/(Decrease) in the average salaries of managerial personnel in FY24 was (6.97)%.”

  reasoning:
    Same entity, measure, period (FY24) and basis, but Annual Report 2023-24 (undated)
    reports 14 per cent and Annual Report 2023-24 (undated) reports (6.97) per cent - a
    149.8% difference with nothing in either frame to account for it.

  escalated -> model says distinct (MANAGERIAL_VS_NON_MANAGERIAL):
    Reading A measures the change in average salaries for employees other than managerial
    personnel (14%), whereas Reading B specifically measures managerial personnel ((6.97)%).
    These represent two distinct employee populations.

CASE 3  An apparent contradiction explained by context
================================================================================================
  The values differ, but so does one coordinate of the frame - the period, the reporting
  basis, or the stage of revision. That coordinate IS the explanation.

  lemma  : Revenue from Operations (Standalone)
  rule   : BASIS_MISMATCH   verdict: reconciled

  reading A  74,540.82 ₹ Million   [FY24]
             Annual Report 2023-24  published None  p.22
             “Revenue from Operations 74,540.82 66,586.61 81,415.38 72,253.01”

  reading B  81,415.38 ₹ Million   [FY24]
             Annual Report 2023-24  published None  p.22
             “Revenue from Operations 74,540.82 66,586.61 81,415.38 72,253.01”

  reasoning:
    Different reporting basis: standalone against consolidated. These are different figures
    by construction, not competing measurements of one figure.
